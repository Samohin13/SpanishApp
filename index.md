# ESPEAK

A mobile app for learning Spanish, designed for Russian speakers. Features include:

- 🎯 60 micro-lessons across A1–B2 (CEFR levels)
- 📚 100 graded reading stories with comprehension quizzes
- 🎮 8 mini-games for vocabulary, grammar, conjugation, pronunciation
- 🤖 AI-powered Spanish tutor (chat with Gemini 1.5 Flash)
- 🃏 Flashcards with SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm
- 🔥 Streaks, XP, achievements, leaderboards
- 🇪🇸 Native-speaker pronunciation with TTS
